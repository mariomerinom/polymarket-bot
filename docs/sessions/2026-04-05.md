## What We Learned

- **GitHub Actions had 0–300s random latency** due to bash sleep loop. WS event-driven engine drops `snapshot_age` from ~300s to ~3s — fills should improve.
- **CLOB pricing was wrong.** `src/trade.py` was using Gamma's `implied_price` instead of the live CLOB orderbook. This was the root cause of adverse selection — the bot was pricing bets against stale data. Fixed in `edc0f832`.
- **ETH live → paper in one day.** ETH went live on Apr 4 and was reverted on Apr 5 after poor results. The momentum signal needs more paper validation before re-promoting.
- **TDD refactoring pattern works.** Write behavioral contracts first (Phase A), then restructure freely (Phase B). `execute_trades()` shrank from 211 → ~65 lines with zero regressions across 440 tests.
- **Bybit at 241ms is sufficient.** Polygon.io dependency dropped entirely — paid subscription no longer needed for candle data.
- **Decision #24 quantified.** 36% fill rate vs 100% WR on expired orders. The orders that expire are the ones that would have won. The CLOB pricing fix is the most direct lever.
