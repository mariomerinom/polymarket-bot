## What We Learned

- **VPS went down at 01:34 UTC due to dual-engine incident.** Second engine started while first was running; shared `TRADING_ENABLED` global got overwritten, silently demoting BTC 5m to PAPER. Root cause: PID lock was missing.
- **SIGALRM is thread-hostile.** It only works from the main thread. The VPS engine dispatches pipelines from worker threads, so every live BTC 5m bet after 6:55PM ET was silently dropped with "signal only works in main thread". Replaced with `ThreadPoolExecutor.result(timeout=...)`.
- **Consecutive loss breaker was deadlocked.** 5 losses on Apr 5 locked trading for 30+ hours. The breaker assumed a win would reset the streak, but it blocks all trades so no win can ever happen. Fixed with 8h auto-reset. Dashboard was also lying: it counted only today's settled orders while the actual breaker counted all recent orders with no date filter.
- **FOK is the right execution model.** GTC passive orders filled adversely (35.7% filled WR vs 76.5% signal WR). FOK orders execute immediately or cancel — no resting in the book.
- **`system_state.py` resolves the dashboard-lie structural problem.** Three places independently computed trading eligibility from raw SQL — now there's one frozen dataclass. Today's incident (dashboard showed healthy while engine deadlocked) is structurally prevented.
- **Apr 5 live P&L: -$100.12 (0W-5L).** 7 orders placed: 5 filled, 2 expired. Paper: +$112.55 at 57.9% WR. WR trend: 90% → 77.8% → 72.7% → 63.4% → 57.9% — degrading but paper still positive.
- **Engine restarted at ~15:00 UTC.** 83 BTC 5m predictions today (15:00–23:35 UTC). Live orders: 1 filled, 4 failed (FOK edge check rejected them — may need calibration).
