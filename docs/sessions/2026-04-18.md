## What We Learned

- The orphan-alert in `pipeline_integrity` was not circuit-breaker-aware: any pipeline with a tripped breaker (expected, correct behavior) was flagged as having orphaned predictions, generating alert noise on every CI run.
- Polymarket shadow resolution requires synthesizing fills from market outcome + mid price rather than intraday candles. The `resolve_shadow_fills_polymarket` approach uses a conservative candle range to avoid overstating fill quality.
- `btc_15m` was the weakest pipeline in the fleet: 33% WR over its last 7 trading days, a declining trend since March. Decision #7 called the sunset April 5 — execution was deferred until today. Historical data is preserved for future retraining.
