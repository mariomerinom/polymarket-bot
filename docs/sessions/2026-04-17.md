## What We Learned

- **Kalshi is architecturally broken, not signal-broken.** Every one of 420 conv>=3 resolved bets was NO-won. The bug is in `ci_run_kalshi.py:223-305` — one momentum signal applied to all markets regardless of strike distance. Preserving historical data; pausing until a market-question parser is built.
- **HIGH_VOL/TRENDING was leaking through perps.** `ci_run_perp.py` only blocked `HIGH_VOL non-trending`; the HV/TRENDING subset was passing at catastrophic WR (eth_bybit 25.7%, sol_bybit 41.2%, doge_bybit 38.5%). The pattern is fleet-wide: HIGH_VOL = bad, regime sub-label doesn't rescue it.
- **Intraday range z-score is the correct gate signal.** Apr 7 (range_z=+2.90 → btc_5m −$193 at 39% WR) and Apr 13 (range_z=+1.83 → btc_5m −$27 at 40% WR) confirm the pattern. The prior gate (#68) failed because it used yesterday's completed row; this version reads today's live candles.
- **ETH 5m is the clear fleet leader today.** 70.6% WR on 17 bets, +$190 P&L — best single-pipeline day in over a week.

## What Got Kicked Forward

- **Kalshi market-question parser** — needs to extract strike price from market slug, compare to current BTC price, and route YES/NO accordingly. No timeline yet; architectural rebuild.
- **Orphaned predictions** — bybit (3), doge_bybit (1), doge_hl (1), eth_bybit (3), eth_hl (3), hl (3) all showing conv>=3 predictions with no corresponding order. Pattern suggests execution is dropping orders silently. Not investigated today.
