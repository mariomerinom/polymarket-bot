## What We Learned

### FAK pilot day 1 — the real result

- **FAK works mechanically.** 90% fill rate (9 of 10 orders) vs pre-FAK 28%. Validated.
- **FAK does NOT fix adverse selection.** 1 win of 9 filled (11% WR). Pre-FAK was 35%.
- **Explanation:** FAK just means we now fill into our losing predictions instead of getting FOK-rejected on them. Execution improvement cannot rescue a negative-EHR signal.
- **Pilot loss:** $159 on 10 orders. Consecutive-loss breaker tripped automatically. System behaved exactly as designed.

### Signal drift is STRUCTURAL, not noise

Compared BTC 5m production WR by regime over two windows:

| Regime | Mar 28 – Apr 13 | Apr 14 – Apr 21 | Δ |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|---|
| MEDIUM_VOL/NEUTRAL | 65.9% (n=123) | **40.4%** (n=57) | **−25pp** |
| HIGH_VOL/TRENDING | 69.0% (n=29) | 42.9% (n=14) | −26pp |

Root cause is visible in daily regime data: past 7 days were **3 of 7 chop days** vs baseline's mix of trending+chop. The 5m momentum signal relies on directional persistence; in chop-dominated markets, 5m streaks are fakeouts, not continuations.

This is the first time we have a clean attribution of signal WR drift to a specific market-regime rotation.

### Lab vs production disconnect — filter audit

- Lab (always-fire) on btc_5m: 57.6% WR over 1,472 bets (14d)
- Production (filtered) on btc_5m: signal EHR -0.082 over 71 bets (7d)

Production filters out ~95% of always-fire candidates via dead hours, HV gate, DOWN+NEUTRAL demotion, conviction boost. Hypothesis: filters are adversely-selecting — keeping the wrong subset. Evidence partially supports this (hour 19 has a dead-hour leak; HV/TRENDING now worse than before), but root cause is the regime drift above, which affects ALL filter buckets.

### VWAP does NOT work on BTC 5m

Lab shows vwap_meanrev at 48-50% WR / +$150 P&L over 14d on btc_5m. Do NOT graduate VWAP to btc_5m (unlike eth_5m where it does show edge). Current momentum path is still the right base signal on BTC when the regime is tradeable.
