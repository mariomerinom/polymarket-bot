## What We Learned

- **Kraken rate-limiting was silently killing consensus.** Every 5-min cycle errored, flooding `loop.log` and setting consensus=None. The conviction +1 boost was never firing. Coinbase has no rate limit.
- **All 906 BTC/ETH/SOL asset_daily rows had NULL z-scores.** `recompute_zscores()` was defined but never called in `record()`. The regime gate was running blind.
- **Data silos block parameter optimization.** Only `predict.py` was storing `indicator_snapshot()`; ETH and perp pipelines stored nothing. Strategy Lab couldn't analyze 1,545 Bybit predictions and 541 ETH 15m predictions.
- **Backfilling is deterministic when you have candles.** Same CSV + TAEngine = exact original indicators. ETH skipped because no historical CSV exists yet.
- **1,426 predictions pre-date CSV range** (before Oct 2024) — those will never have indicator metadata.
