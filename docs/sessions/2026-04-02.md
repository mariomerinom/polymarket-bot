## What We Learned

- **Fill rate is the #1 P&L lever.** 53% fill rate on BTC (9/17 submitted). All 8 expired orders predicted correctly — $165 in missed profit. If those filled, BTC P&L would be +$169 instead of +$3.74.
- **ETH books are too thin for $25 bets.** Wins were $1-2 (book-limited), losses were full $25. Asymmetric damage: -$65.55 on 9 orders.
- **VWAP promotion was based on a misread stat.** "42/50 at 78.6%" was the RSI/OBV shadow aggregate, not VWAP-specific. Actual VWAP: 5W-12L, 29.4% WR. Caught before BTC money was lost (zero BTC shadow predictions existed).
- **P&L reconciliation with Polymarket CSV is within $0.39.** DB and on-chain agree. Three orders show size discrepancies (DB records intended $25, CLOB filled $20 or less).
- **20 early orders failed due to API bug** (`order_type` kwarg removed in py-clob-client update). Fixed before order #37.
- **Order splitting is normal** — single $25 orders arrive on-chain as 1-6 fills.
