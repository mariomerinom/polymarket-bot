## What We Learned

1. **HIGH_VOL non-trending is the #1 regime killer across all pipelines.** BTC: 54.8% WR on 126 bets. ETH: 40.7% on 27. Bybit: 40.8% on 98. Momentum streaks in high-vol choppy markets are noise that reverses. The longer the streak, the worse it performs (streak 4+ went 20.8% on Apr 7-8).

2. **BTC 15m is the exception.** HIGH_VOL performs at 64.3% WR on 56 bets at the 15m timeframe — different dynamics. The gate correctly excludes 15m via `loose_mode`.

3. **Funding rate has no edge at 5-minute scale.** 18-month backtest on 37K momentum signals: extreme funding WR = 49.0%, normal funding WR = 47.4%. The signal is real at 8h windows (89% WR) but completely absent at 5m. Regime matters, funding doesn't — at our timeframe.

4. **Kalshi has been accumulating garbage data for a week.** 3,871 "resolved" predictions were hash-based random (MD5 of market ID % 2). All WR data was meaningless noise at ~48.7%. Fresh accumulation starts now.

5. **The optimization tracker measures pipeline-wide WR, not per-optimization WR.** All 11 stale optimizations showed identical numbers because they shared the same pipeline baseline, not because they were individually measured. Important nuance for future experiment design.

6. **BTC drawdown Apr 7-8 was regime-driven.** Two days at 40.5% WR (74 bets, -$432). Market shifted from trending/low-vol to HIGH_VOL/NEUTRAL. The conv filter was selecting the strongest momentum signals — which were exactly the most likely to reverse. Circuit breaker tripped correctly at $317/$300.
