## What We Learned

1. **Kelly Criterion on live data is dramatic.** Quarter Kelly on 221 resolved bets would have turned $5K into $166K vs our fixed-sizing $3,735 P&L. But this is theoretical — order book depth caps practical sizing.

2. **CLOB liquidity is the real ceiling.** First live order book query showed $206 max bet at 2% slippage, 30 depth levels, 1.44% spread. Our conv=4 bets ($200) are already at the boundary. Scaling beyond this requires maker orders (0% fee) or splitting across markets.

3. **15m CI was 100% dead.** Every 15m workflow run was failing silently because `git pull --rebase` crashed on unstaged changes from concurrent 5m pushes. Fix: `git stash --include-untracked` before pull. Both workflows now patched.

4. **Friend's Hurst branch: hold off.** External contributor's Hurst exponent regime detector showed +3% WR improvement on 15m backtest, but absolute WR is still 45.7% (below coinflip). Not ready to merge.

5. **Peer review raised 4 valid concerns.** Conv=5 sizing risk, ATR/volatility filter, candle-to-resolution timing drift, Brier score surfacing. All registered as decision #10-13 for tracking.

6. **NEUTRAL regime is where the money hides.** 41.2% Kelly fraction in NEUTRAL vs 17.5% in TRENDING on live data. Counter-intuitive — low-volatility consensus periods have the strongest edge.

7. **Multi-asset expansion is viable but risky.** SOL and ETH 5m/15m markets exist on Polymarket. Plan documented with 11 known breakage points and frozen-files rule to protect the BTC 5m winner.
