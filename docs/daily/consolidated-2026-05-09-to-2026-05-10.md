## 5. Promotion Read

**BTC 5m remains the cleanest signal candidate, but not promotion-ready.** It won May 10 with +$78.72 on 5 bets and finished the two-day window positive at 56.3% WR, but execution EHR remained negative and the infrastructure report still shows severe true orderbook-age p95. Promote only after execution freshness and orphan classification gates are clean.

**Kalshi is useful but unstable.** It was the best May 9 pipeline and the worst May 10 pipeline. The two-day net is barely positive, which argues for cohort review before sizing or production promotion. The question is not whether it can win; it is whether the winning regimes are separable from the giveback regimes.

**SOL and DOGE were strong but too thin.** Both assets had excellent two-day win rates and positive P&L, but the bet counts are small. Keep them in monitoring and use them for cohort discovery, not promotion.

**ETH remains blocked.** ETH 5m was profitable over the two-day window, but ETH as an asset lost -$238.55 and ETH bybit/HL both carried negative rolling signal EHR. Treat ETH 5m separately from ETH perps/HL, and do not use the asset-level read to bless all ETH exposure.
